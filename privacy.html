MMäx

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Mäx collects no personal data. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDK that reports anything about you. This policy exists because the App Store requires every app to publish one, not because there is much to disclose.

What the app stores

Everything the app remembers is written to storage on your own device:

  • the language you are learning and the language you read explanations in
  • your level and daily goal
  • which words you have saved or marked as known, and how well you know them
  • which stories you have read
  • how many minutes you have studied on each day, which is what the streak, the week chart and the six-month heatmap are drawn from
  • anything you type into the Tutor screen, kept so the conversation is still there when you come back

None of it is sent anywhere. It is not backed up to us, because there is no “us” to back it up to — there is no server, no account and no network request in the app.

Microphone and speech recognition

The speaking drill is the only feature that uses the microphone, and only while you are actively using it. When you tap the microphone button, iOS listens and converts what you said to text so the app can compare it with the sentence on screen.

Where iOS supports on-device recognition for your language, that conversion happens entirely on your phone. For some languages iOS instead sends the audio to Apple’s own speech recognition service; that transfer is made by iOS, is governed by Apple’s privacy policy, and is the same mechanism used by dictation on the system keyboard.

In neither case does Mäx record, store or transmit audio. The recognised text is compared, scored and discarded. If you decline the microphone permission, the speaking drill explains that and the rest of the app carries on working.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, so there is no children’s data to handle differently.

Deleting your data

Two ways, both immediate and complete:

  • Settings → Reset progress inside the app clears saved words, streak, history, XP and the tutor conversation.
  • Deleting the app removes everything, since all of it lives inside the app’s own storage.

There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing to request from us and nothing to revoke.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here and the date at the top updated. A change that materially affects what is collected would ship alongside an app update describing it.
